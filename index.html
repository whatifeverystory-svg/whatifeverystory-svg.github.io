What If

Animated stories that happen to you

What if a robot replaced you, and nobody noticed? What if your body ran on Mars time? What if every step paid you a dollar?

Every story on What If happens to you. We take one impossible "what if" and follow it all the way to the twist. Kai stands in for you. Each episode is built on one real fact from the science behind it, and the source is linked in the description.

The stories are fiction. The facts are real. A new episode every 3 days.

About the What If app

"What If" is also the name of a small private tool the channel owner uses to manage this one YouTube channel. It reads the channel's analytics and, only after the owner approves each change, updates its own videos' details (titles, descriptions, tags, category and publish schedule). It is not offered to the public and does not access anyone else's account.
